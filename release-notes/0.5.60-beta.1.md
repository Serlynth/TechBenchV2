# TechBench Inventory Beta 0.5.60-beta.1

- Adds a workstation-local Stable/Inventory Beta update-channel toggle in Settings and on the connection screen.
- Inventory Beta testers can return to Stable through the normal updater without downloading a separate installer.
- The selected channel is remembered locally and applies immediately to update checks.
- Continues the SQL-backed Inventory Board beta introduced in 0.5.58-beta.1.
